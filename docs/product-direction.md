# nerdPRM Product Direction

This note expands the public product framing used in the main showcase README.

## Core idea

nerdPRM appears to sit between project intake and delivery execution. The interesting product angle is that AI assistance is treated as part of execution planning rather than as a detached chatbot.

## Product themes

- structure the intake of work
- turn project requests into execution paths
- keep team visibility and knowledge capture connected
- use AI where it supports planning and delivery clarity
- make Arabic-first product language feel native

## Why the current public layer is still useful

Even with only the landing screen safely published, the public layer already communicates:

- who the product is for
- what it is trying to improve
- how AI is positioned in the story

That is better than publishing weak or half-working internal screens just to increase screenshot count.
